# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — first playable (Dev)

- Catalog of 4,306 procedurally named species across 30 sets, built from the sprite library
- Seven finish tiers, with serialized 3D Pop (/50), Living Foil (/10) and Factory Misprint (1/1)
- Pack opening: tear, card trick, rarity tells and tiered reveals, haul summary with bulk quick-sell
- Binder with set pages, silhouettes, completion medals and an inventory with bulk tools
- Player market with fees, a market value model, price history, movers and grails
- Profiles with showcase pedestals, themes, animated banners, titles and a badge wall
- Hall with leaderboards, a live feed and the misprint registry
- Realtime ticker over websocket; NPC collectors for local and dev worlds
- Anti-abuse: a new-account trade guard and a registration rate limit
